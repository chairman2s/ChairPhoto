Sliders work at once on the preview; the moment the RAW is ready the stage swaps to it, same framing, same zoom, and your adjustments re-render on the real pixels. The swap is visible — the RAW does not look like the camera's JPEG, which is the point — so stepping through a set prepares the next photos in the background, and a photo prepared once opens prepared the next time.
